Share card video backgrounds

Put videos here named 1.mp4, 2.mp4, 3.mp4 ... with no gaps in the numbers.
Kixo finds them by counting up from 1 and stops at the first missing number.

- Format: MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio). Landscape works best (16:9, e.g. 1280x720).
- Keep each one short (5 to 15 seconds) and small (under about 5 MB) so cards open fast.
- The music plays with the card; users can turn the sound off.
- Only use videos and music you have the rights to (your own, or royalty-free with a
  licence that allows this use).
