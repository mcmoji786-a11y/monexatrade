# Kixo: ranking plan and backlinks

Goal: rank for "Solana memecoin trading bot", "AI memecoin trading bot", "memecoin trading
terminal", "pump.fun trading bot", "rug pull checker" and similar searches.

Backlinks cannot be generated by code; they are earned on other sites. Never buy link
packages, private blog networks or spam comments: Google penalises them and the domain
can be removed from search. Below is the order that works.

## 0. Day 1 (as soon as the domain is live)
1. **Google Search Console** (search.google.com/search-console): add the domain, submit
   `https://YOURDOMAIN/sitemap.xml`. Check "Rich results" for the FAQ.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools** (bing.com/webmasters): import from Google Search Console.
3. Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the real domain in both apps (waitlist and app), so the
   canonical URL, sitemap and share cards use it.
4. Test the share card: paste an invite link into https://cards-dev.twitter.com/validator
   or just post it on X; and https://www.opengraph.xyz.

## 1. Profiles that link to the site (free, high trust)
- X profile (website field + pinned post), Telegram channel description, YouTube channel
  "links", TikTok/Instagram bio, LinkedIn company page.
- **Crunchbase** company profile (also helps investors check you).
- **GitHub** organisation `kixo` with a README linking to the site.
- **Medium**, **Substack** or **dev.to** account (for articles, see section 4).

## 3. Communities (traffic first, links second)
- Reddit: r/solana, r/SolanaMemeCoins, r/CryptoCurrency. Share useful posts (for example a
  real rug breakdown with Kixo's labels), not ads. Read each subreddit's self-promotion rules.
- Hacker News "Show HN" on launch day, with how the safety score works.
- Discord and Telegram trading groups: offer free early access to admins.

## 4. Content that earns links (the strongest long-term lever)
The main app already has 6 guides at /learn. Grow to 20-30, one a week, each answering a
real search. Ideas:
- How to spot a rug pull on pump.fun before you buy (with screenshots of real labels)
- What is a bundled launch on Solana, and how to see it
- Best Solana memecoin trading bots compared (honest table, Kixo included)
- How AI trading bots for memecoins work, and their limits
- Sniper bots explained: what they do to new coins
- How to read a memecoin's holder distribution
- Copy trading memecoins: why most copiers lose (exit liquidity)
- pump.fun vs letsbonk.fun vs Bags: how launches differ
- Weekly "rugs of the week" with on-chain data (journalists and creators cite data)
Publish on the site first; post a summary on Medium/Substack/dev.to linking back.

Data people link to: a monthly "State of Solana memecoins" report from Kixo's own data
(launches per day, share that graduated, share with bundled launches). Journalists and
creators link to original numbers.
